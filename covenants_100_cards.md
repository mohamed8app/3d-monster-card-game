# 📜 100 Vows & Pacts Cards Master Database (Final Clean Edition)
### Deck Game — Official Strategic Expansion Specification

## ⚖️ The 5 Universal Covenant Rules
1. **Unique Covenant Rule**: Exactly 1 active Covenant on the Altar at any time.
2. **Turn Timing**: Vow penalties resolve at Turn Start before card draw.
3. **The 1-Mana Floor**: No cost reduction can ever reduce a card below 1 Mana.
4. **Self-Inflicted Cost**: Health paid for vows cannot be prevented or mitigated by armor or shields.
5. **Non-Recursion**: Reanimated monsters do not trigger on-summon covenant pacts.
